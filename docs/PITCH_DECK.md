# MediMitra AI — Pitch Deck Outline (10 Slides)

## Slide 1: Title & Tagline
- **Project**: MediMitra AI
- **Tagline**: "Your AI Health Companion — Speaks Your Language, Understands Your Needs"
- **CodeStorm 2026 #2 Submission**

## Slide 2: The Healthcare Access Crisis
- 70% of rural population lacks timely medical access.
- 1:10,000 doctor shortage in rural districts.
- Severe language & literacy barriers in conventional English apps.
- Delayed diagnosis leads to preventable mortality.

## Slide 3: The MediMitra Solution
- Multilingual, voice-first AI health companion.
- Works 100% offline via PWA.
- Multimodal AI: Triage symptoms by voice, scan pill packaging, analyze skin wounds.

## Slide 4: Key AI Capabilities
1. Voice-First Multilingual Symptom Checker (Gemini 2.0).
2. Pill & Prescription OCR Vision (Gemini Vision + openFDA).
3. Skin & Wound Care Vision Triage.
4. Emergency SOS & GPS Hospital Locator.
5. Teleconsultation Room with AI Live Translation.

## Slide 5: Technical Stack
- **Frontend**: Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Lucide icons, Framer Motion, Recharts.
- **AI/ML**: Google Gemini 2.0 Flash, Web Speech API (STT & TTS), openFDA API.
- **Backend**: FastAPI, Python, RAG medical database.
- **PWA**: Offline Service Worker.

## Slide 6: Inclusive Accessibility Design
- High contrast WCAG AAA mode.
- Font size scaling (A- / A / A+).
- Voice read-out of all diagnoses for illiterate or elderly users.

## Slide 7: Real World Impact Metrics
- Serves potential 900M+ rural population.
- Reduces preliminary triage diagnostic wait time from hours to < 2 seconds.
- 10+ Indic languages supported.

## Slide 8: Business & Sustainability Model
- Free tier for rural individuals & primary health centers.
- B2G & B2B integration with state health departments & tele-clinics.

## Slide 9: Competitive Advantage Matrix
- MediMitra AI vs. Generic Chatbots: Multilingual voice-first, offline PWA, pill vision, emergency SOS built-in.

## Slide 10: Future Roadmap & Call to Action
- IoT smart pulse-oximeter integration.
- Direct ambulance integration with state 108 emergency network.
- Join us in building accessible healthcare for everyone.
