## 🎛️ PART 3: RECORDING & AUDIO SETUP CHECKLIST

### 1. OBS Studio Screen Recording Setup
- **Base Canvas Resolution**: 1920x1080 (1080p)
- **Output Resolution**: 1920x1080 @ 60 FPS
- **Video Bitrate**: 12,000 Kbps (CBR)
- **Audio Sample Rate**: 48 kHz / 24-bit Stereo
- **Mic Filters**: Add **Noise Suppression** (RNNoise) + **Limiter** (-2.0 dB) in OBS.

### 2. Browser Environment Preparation
- Open Chrome in **Incognito Window** or clean guest profile.
- Zoom setting: **100%** (Full HD 1920x1080 viewport).
- Hide browser bookmarks bar (`Cmd + Shift + B` on Mac).
- Pre-open live app: **`https://dhanya-medimitra.vercel.app`**

### 3. Background Music Recommendations (Royalty-Free)
- **Track Style**: Modern Cinematic Tech / Uplifting Ambient Healthcare.
- **Top Picks**:
  - *"Inspiration Tech"* by Pixabay Music
  - *"Cinematic Ambient Medical"* by Epidemic Sound
- **Volume Mix Level**: Background music at **-22 dB**, Voiceover at **-3 dB** (voice must remain 100% crisp and clear).
