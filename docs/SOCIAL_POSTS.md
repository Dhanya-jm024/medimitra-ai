## 💼 1. LINKEDIN ANNOUNCEMENT POST

**Headline**: 🚨 700 Million people in rural India have 1 doctor for every 10,000 citizens. We built an AI to solve this.

I’m thrilled to introduce **MediMitra AI (ಮೇಡಿಮಿತ್ರ AI)** — our flagship submission for **CodeStorm 2026 #2**! 🏥✨

Healthcare shouldn't be a privilege determined by your geography or language. In rural regions, over 70% of patients face critical health delays because they cannot read English prescriptions or access urban hospitals.

**MediMitra AI** is a multilingual, voice-first AI health companion designed to democratize medical triage for rural & elderly users:

🎙️ **Voice-First Triage**: Speak symptoms naturally in 10+ Indic languages (Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, English) with instant audio readouts.  
💊 **Pill Vision OCR**: Camera scan pill strips for dosages & openFDA drug safety warnings.  
🩺 **Skin & Wound AI**: Visual dermatological triage & first-aid steps.  
🚨 **Global GPS Emergency SOS**: High-precision satellite tracking with 1-tap emergency dispatch & nearest trauma center routing.  
♿ **Elderly Accessibility**: WCAG AAA High Contrast mode & dynamic font scaling.  
🌐 **100% Offline PWA**: Works seamlessly in zero-connectivity rural dead zones.

Built with **Next.js 14, Google Gemini 2.0 Flash & Vision, FastAPI, Supabase, and OpenStreetMap**.
