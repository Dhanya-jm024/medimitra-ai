# MediMitra AI — 2-Minute Judge Demo Video Script

"Let's switch our language to Hindi or Kannada. I'll tap the microphone and speak my symptoms out loud in Hindi: 'मुझे तेज बुखार और सिरदर्द है'."
"Within 2 seconds, Gemini 2.0 analyzes the symptoms, returns a 94% confidence triage, lists home remedies, and reads out the diagnosis audibly!"

## [0:45 - 1:10] Feature 2: Medicine & Pill Scanner
"Next, elderly users often struggle to read fine print on medicine strips. With MediMitra AI, we simply snap a photo of the pill strip. Gemini Vision extracts the medicine name, dosage, active ingredients, and checks safety warnings via openFDA."

"In critical emergencies, pressing the floating Red SOS button extracts GPS coordinates, broadcasts SMS alerts to registered family members, and lists the 5 nearest hospitals with 1-tap phone dialing."

"We designed MediMitra AI for everyone: elderly users can scale font size with one click or activate High Contrast WCAG AAA mode. When network drops, our PWA offline engine keeps local triage and health records running."
