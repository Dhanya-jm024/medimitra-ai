# 🔮 MediMitra AI — Product Roadmap (2026 - 2027)

> **Vision**: Bringing inclusive, multilingual, voice-first AI healthcare to 900+ million underserved rural citizens.

---

## 📌 Phase 1: Q1 2026 (Current Release - CodeStorm 2026 #2)
- [x] **Multilingual Voice-First Triage**: Web Speech STT/TTS in 10+ Indic languages (English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu).
- [x] **Gemini 2.0 Vision OCR**: Camera scan pill strips & prescription labels.
- [x] **Global Satellite GPS SOS**: Continuous GPS tracking, reverse geocoding, OpenStreetMap radar, and Twilio SMS dispatch.
- [x] **WCAG AAA Accessibility**: Dynamic font scaling (**A- / A / A / A+**) and High Contrast mode.
- [x] **PWA Offline Support**: 100% offline capability for zero-network rural zones.

---

## 🚀 Phase 2: Q2 2026 (Ayushman Bharat & IoT Sync)
- [ ] **Ayushman Bharat (ABDM) Integration**: Link patient profiles directly to India's ABDM digital health ID stack.
- [ ] **Bluetooth IoT Sync**: Connect low-cost pulse oximeters, blood pressure cuffs, and glucometers via Web Bluetooth API.
- [ ] **Expanded Language Suite**: Add Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati, Punjabi, and Malayalam.

---

## 🏥 Phase 3: Q3 2026 (Telemedicine & Pharmacy Network)
- [ ] **Direct Doctor Video Rooms**: Enable 1-tap teleconsultation with certified rural doctors with live AI real-time voice translation.
- [ ] **WhatsApp & Offline SMS Gateway**: Twilio WhatsApp bot for feature phones without web browsers.
- [ ] **Jan Aushadhi Generic Pharmacy Finder**: Direct routing to nearest government generic medicine stores.

---

## 🌐 Phase 4: Q4 2026 & Beyond (Global Rural Health Expansion)
- [ ] **International Rural Expansion**: Adapt voice models for Southeast Asia and Sub-Saharan Africa.
- [ ] **Voice-Enabled Solar Hardware Pods**: Standalone offline hardware kiosks for Gram Panchayats and rural PHCs.
